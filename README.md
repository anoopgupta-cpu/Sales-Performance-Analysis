# Sales-Performance-Analysis
Sales data analysis and Power BI dashboard project
Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI with sales data imported from Microsoft Excel.

The objective is to provide a visual overview of sales performance through key performance indicators (KPIs), charts, and business insights.

🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance.
- Analyze total revenue and order volume.
- Understand customer and product performance.
- Explore geographical sales distribution.
- Identify key sales trends and patterns.

🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft Power BI

📈 Key Performance Indicators

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Unique Customers
- Average Order Value

🔍 Dashboard Components

1. Revenue and order performance KPIs.
2. Quantity vs Revenue Analysis.
3. Geographical Sales Performance.
4. Product-wise Revenue Analysis.
5. Country-wise Revenue Analysis.
6. Key Business Insights.


##Business Insights

The dashboard has provided information that shows:

1) Revenue = Total revenue was approx. £37.81K from 87 orders.
2) Customers = 73 different customers were used.
3) AOV = Approx. £434.61 average order value.
4) UK = The UK was the highest revenue generating country.
5) Products = The Black Record Cover Frame generated the highest amount of revenue.


##Business Recommendations

Look at revenue from different geographic regions.
Look at product level sales.
Look at average order value and look at customer buying patterns.
Look into ways to increase sales and improve customer retention.
